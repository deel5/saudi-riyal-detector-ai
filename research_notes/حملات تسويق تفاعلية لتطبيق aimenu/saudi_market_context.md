# Saudi/GCC Market Context for an Interactive Mystery Campaign (aimenu)

Research date: 2026-11-17. Method note: these notes rest mostly on web-search result summaries. ## 1. Notable Saudi/GCC interactive, gamified, ARG-like or treasure-hunt campaigns

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia already has well-known precedents for treasure-hunt and puzzle mechanics at national scale (Riyadh Season Treasure Hunt) and in food delivery (HungerStation x Snapchat AR treasure hunt; the "Into the Snapverse" lens). I found no documented true ARG ("lost phone" or found-footage style) by a Saudi delivery brand, so the format looks open but untested locally.

### Cited Findings
- **Riyadh Season Treasure Hunt (2023):** launched Nov 7, 2023. UNIT9 built the app in Unity for iOS and Android. Mechanics included code-breaking, deciphering media content, QR-code scanning for hidden clues and AR steps. It drew more than 100,000 downloads, and the top 10 solvers went to a live finale — [UNIT9 project page](https://unit9.com/project/riyadh-season-treasure-hunt); [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/node/4648756); [Ajel: prizes SAR 6M](https://english.ajel.sa/news/saudi-arabia/riyadh-season-treasure-hunt-starts-with-prizes-sar6-million)
- The grand-prize amount conflicts between sources: UNIT9 materials say USD 1.6M, while Saudi Gazette reports a Swiss winner taking USD 1M (SAR 3.75M) — [UNIT9 case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/unit9/case-study/riyadh-season-launches-global-treasure-hunt-with-unit9-driving-100k-downloads-and-a-16m-prize); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/640352)
- **HungerStation x Snapchat AR treasure hunt (2024):** a sponsored digital treasure hunt on Snapchat, billed by both companies as the first of its kind in Saudi Arabia. It was part of HungerStation's rebrand — [Arab News](https://arab.news/pwcyu)
- **HungerStation "Into the Snapverse" (Carat MENA / Delivery Hero):** an AR lens aimed at Saudi youth to "go beyond top-of-mind awareness". HungerStation was reportedly the first advertiser globally to launch that lens format, and the campaign won Bronze at MMA Smarties KSA — [dentsu case study](https://www.dentsu.com/ae/en/our-work/hungerstation-and-snapchat-case-study); [MMA Global](https://mmaglobal.com/node/44040)
- **HungerStation x Fananees (MBC) Ramadan 2024:** a character and IP partnership rather than a game. Dentsu reported +35.4% orders vs Ramadan 2023, +24.6% acquisition and +2 pts brand favourability, and the campaign won Smarties Silver — [dentsu](https://www.dentsu.com/ae/en/our-work/fananees-and-hungerstation-case-study); [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/hungerstation-and-fananees-team-up-to-build-emotional-connections-in-ramadan/)
- **HungerStation "Tip with Time" (Wunderman Thompson/VML):** a first-day-of-Ramadan push notification asking customers to "tip" riders with time rather than money. It shows push notifications being used as a storytelling device, and it appears on Dubai Lynx / LoveTheWork — [VML](https://www.vml.com/work/tip-with-time); [LoveTheWork](https://www.lovethework.com/work-awards/campaigns/tip-time-1591340)
- **STC "Baity Battle" (Ramadan 2023):** a Fortnite island featured on Fortnite's Discover page during the first days of Ramadan, with weekly updates through the month — [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/wunderman-thompson-went-all-guns-blazing-in-ksa-for-ramadan/)
- **VML "Eidiya 18":** a Saudi Eid campaign listed by VML (mechanics not retrieved) — [VML](https://www.vml.com/work/eidiya-18)
- **Riyadh Season on Roblox (Oct 20, 2025):** a branded world with a treasure hunt for golden tickets that players redeem for in-game items — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/saudi-arabia-launches-riyadh-season-themed-entertainment-experiences-on-roblox); [Branding in Asia](https://www.brandinginasia.com/bringing-saudi-arabias-riyadh-season-to-roblox)
- **Jeddah Season scavenger hunt** around historic monuments — [Eye of Riyadh](https://eyeofriyadh.com/news/details/jeddah-season-launches-scavenger-hunt-to-explore-jeddah-historical-monuments)
- Campaign ME publishes an annual "top 20 digital and integrated campaigns" list for 2025, a useful benchmark set (contents not retrieved) — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/the-annual-top-20-digital-and-integrated-campaigns-from-2025/)

### Inferences
- Treasure hunts and puzzles are a familiar, government-endorsed format in KSA because of Riyadh Season, so audiences should understand the mechanic. A "lost phone" ARG would add narrative novelty on top of a format people already know.
- HungerStation owns the "AR treasure hunt on Snapchat" space in food delivery. aimenu should set itself apart through story and AI interaction (for example, the phone's AI assistant as a character, or menu clues) rather than repeating a Snap lens hunt.
- The Fananees result points to Ramadan and characters or local IP as the strongest proven lever for delivery orders.

### Gaps
- I found no documented interactive or gamified campaigns from Jahez, Keeta, ToYou, Mrsool, Careem, Al Baik or Kudu. The searches returned nothing specific, and Dubai Lynx/Effie winner lists were not reachable.
- I found no Saudi "lost phone" or found-footage ARG precedent. Global examples (e.g., Why So Serious-style ARGs) were out of scope.
- Results metrics for the HungerStation Snapchat treasure hunt (participants, orders) were not retrieved.

## 2. Saudi social media usage 2025-2026 and platform fit; Snapchat AR adoption

### Takeaway
Saudi Arabia is close to fully online, at about 99% internet penetration. Snapchat (about 25-26M users, very high daily usage) and TikTok (ad reach above 100% of adults, the highest or among the highest globally) are the main storytelling and youth platforms. YouTube has the widest reach, Instagram covers about half the population, and X serves conversation and news. Saudi Arabia shows some of Snap's highest AR engagement rates, but no Saudi AR user count was found.

### Cited Findings
- DataReportal (data as of Oct 2025) puts cellular connections at 48.7M at end-2025. Snapchat's ad reach was 25.3M (72.9% of the population) in late 2025 — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Saudi Arabia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-saudi-arabia) (via search summary)
- The latest DataReportal page (labelled Digital 2027, data Oct 2026) lists 35.0M internet users (99.0% penetration), 44.8M social media user identities (about 127% of a 35.3M population; identities ≠ unique people) — [DataReportal Digital 2027 Saudi Arabia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-saudi-arabia) (via search summary; data vintage needs checking)
- Snap CEO Evan Spiegel said at FII9 (Oct 2025) that Snapchat has 26M users in Saudi Arabia, in a country of about 33M — [Saudi Gazette](https://cdn.saudigazette.com.sa/article/656090/SAUDI-ARABIA/Snapchat-reaches-26-million-users-in-Saudi-Arabia-says-CEO-Evan-Spiegel-at-FII9)
- Snapchat is dominant among Saudi youth, with 90% of users aged 13-34 — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/snapchat-dominates-among-saudi-youth-with-90-of-users-aged-13-34-1.500043687). "If it's happening in the Kingdom, chances are it's on Snapchat" — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2548521/media). Snap's regional lead claims Saudi users open the app more than 50 times a day (older company claim, same source family).
- Snapchat AR lenses are used about 8B times a day globally, with 350M+ daily AR users (Q3 2025). Saudi Arabia is reported among the highest AR engagement markets — [SocialPilot Snapchat stats](https://socialpilot.co/blog/snapchat-statistics) (secondary aggregator)
- Snap launched an "App Power Pack" for Saudi advertisers aimed at up to 25% app-install growth — [The Brand Berries](https://thebrandberries.com/?p=24968)
- TikTok's ad reach was about 138% of the adult population (DataReportal 2025), and Saudi Arabia topped the global ranking for TikTok reach in Jan 2026 — [AKchabar](https://www.akchabar.kg/en/news/saudovskaya-araviya-vozglavila-rejting-stran-po-okhvatu-tiktok-jjykkywjkaakfklr); [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1299829/tiktok-penetration-worldwide-by-country)
- YouTube had 27.2M users (79.4%) per DataReportal 2025 — [DataReportal Digital 2025 Saudi Arabia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-saudi-arabia). Other secondary sources cite about 32.5M potential reach.
- Instagram figures vary: about 20.0M (51.7%) in July 2026 per NapoleonCat — [NapoleonCat](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/instagram-users-in-saudi_arabia/2026/03/); 16.9M ad reach per DataReportal 2025.
- X: no reliable 2026 figure found. One aggregator gives about 14.5M, labelled "directional" — [WifiTalents](https://wifitalents.com/saudi-advertising-industry-statistics/)
- Saudi digital ad spend rose about 25% YoY to about USD 300M (12 months to July 2026) per Sensor Tower, via a LinkedIn post (low-confidence source) — [LinkedIn](https://sa.linkedin.com/in/mohammadalgohary)
- AI is reshaping GCC social platforms from Snapchat to WhatsApp — [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/insights/from-snapchat-to-whatsapp-how-ai-is-rewriting-gcc-social-media/)

### Inferences
- For a "lost phone" ARG, the content naturally lives in Snapchat-like formats (Stories, chats, Snap Map-style clues) and TikTok (short videos that seed the mystery and drive community sleuthing). X suits public clue-solving threads and hashtags, while WhatsApp-style chat screenshots suit the phone's interface. YouTube suits long-form reveals.
- A web-based "phone UI" microsite or an in-app experience would let users "browse" the phone, with social posts as the entry points.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative WhatsApp penetration figure for KSA for 2025-2026, and no reliable X figure.
- I found no Saudi-specific AR lens usage count or Snapchat Ramadan usage data.
- The DataReportal pages could not be fetched directly, so platform-by-platform 2026 ad reach (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X) is not confirmed from the primary source.

## 3. Saudi food delivery market size and competitive landscape 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The market is a three-player oligopoly (HungerStation, Keeta, Jahez, together above 90% share) that Keeta's subsidy-led entry (Sept 2024) has upended. Uber's pending USD 14.8B acquisition of Delivery Hero (HungerStation's owner) sets up an Uber-vs-Keeta battle. Market-size estimates vary widely (USD 2-6.4B for 2025).

### Cited Findings
- Momentum Works estimates end-2025 shares at HungerStation about 40%, Keeta about 33% and Jahez above 20% — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2652050/business-economy)
- Earlier Redseer data had Keeta at 8% of GMV within two quarters of launch, rising to about 10%, taking share mainly from smaller players. Keeta, Jahez and HungerStation hold above 90% combined — [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/keeta-has-gained-10-market-share-in-ksa-whats-next/); [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/ksa-food-aggregators-leap-ahead-in-growth-journey/) (via search summary). The shares conflict with Momentum Works because of timing and method (GMV vs orders).
- Keeta launched in Saudi Arabia in Sept 2024 and passed 150M orders in 2025. Jahez recorded about 68.7M orders in 9M-2025 with an average order value of SAR 63.2 — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1886411)
- In Keeta's 2025 review, fast food was the most-ordered cuisine nationwide, followed by Arabic cuisine — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/press-release/companies-news/keetas-2025-year-in-review-shows-how-saudi-arabia-ordered-food-and-saved-more-wxa1989e); [QSR Media](https://qsrmedia.com/research/news/fast-food-dominates-saudi-arabias-2025-delivery-scene)
- Uber agreed to buy Delivery Hero (owner of HungerStation and talabat) for USD 14.8B. Closing is expected in H2 2027, subject to clearances. The Saudi General Authority for Competition was reviewing the deal as of Sept 24, 2026. Analysts expect the main fight to be Uber vs Keeta — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2651262/business-economy); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/cpi-posts/saudi-arabia-reviews-ubers-14-8-billion-delivery-hero-takeover/); [Entrepreneur ME](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/business-news/uber-agrees-us14-8-billion-acquisition-of-delivery-hero-expanding-middle-east-delivery-business)
- Mobile food delivery orders grew about 30% in the UAE and KSA in H1 2025 (Syrve data), with a spike in March 2025 (Ramadan) and later evening peaks — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business-technology-review/food-delivery-goes-mobile-30-growth-in-uae-and-saudi-arabia-in-2025); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/aggregators-dominate-food-delivery-with-mobile-orders-up-by-30-in-the-uae-and-saudi-arabia-in-h1-2025-tka7w38s)
- Market size estimates conflict: about USD 6.4B in 2025, growing at 11.31% CAGR to about USD 12.2B by 2031 (one report) versus about USD 2.01B in 2025 (another) — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/ksa-food-delivery-market); [ReportCubes](https://www.thereportcubes.com/report-store/online-food-delivery-platforms-market-saudi-arabia)
- Redseer notes Saudi q-commerce regulations are "ending the subsidy era" — [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/saudi-q-com-regulations-ending-the-subsidy-era/)

### Inferences
- A new AI menu/delivery entrant cannot win on subsidies against Keeta or Uber. Earned attention (ARG, puzzles, AI novelty) and restaurant-side value (AI menus) are more realistic levers.
- Fast food plus Arabic cuisine dominance suggests clue design and prizes around popular QSR and local dishes.

### Gaps
- No audited market size exists. HungerStation does not publish Saudi operating data, and Jahez full-year 2025 results were not retrieved.
- I found no 2026 market-share update after the Uber deal announcement, and no data on ToYou, Mrsool or Careem food shares.

## 4. Cultural factors: seasonality, humor, influencers, privacy and family norms

### Takeaway
Ramadan is the peak season for delivery and campaigns, followed by Eid, National Day (Sept 23) and Founding Day (Feb 22). National occasions carry strict official rules on symbols, especially the flag. The "lost phone" concept touches a real legal and cultural sensitivity: snooping in someone's phone is a crime in KSA. The concept should frame the phone as a fictional character's phone that was deliberately "left for you" (consensual and staged), and never imply that real people's private data is being exposed.

### Cited Findings
- Ramadan drives order spikes as eating shifts to after sunset. Jahez saw a 70% surge in Ramadan orders (2024), per Arthur D. Little — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2593733); March 2025 mobile order spike — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business-technology-review/food-delivery-goes-mobile-30-growth-in-uae-and-saudi-arabia-in-2025)
- Ramadan is e-commerce's "peak season" in KSA — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2481716)
- Brands use Ramadan for emotional and character-led storytelling (HungerStation x Fananees; Tip with Time; STC Fortnite) — see section 1 sources.
- National Day 2026 has an official brand identity, "Our Pride Is Our Nature" (فخرنا طبعنا), with guidelines for advertising use — [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/saudi-reveals-national-day-2026-brand-identity-built-around-our-pride-is-our-nature/)
- The Saudi flag must not be used as a trademark or for commercial advertising, printed on disposable materials, or carry logos or drawings — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/national-day-saudi-arabia-issues-strict-guidelines-for-using-national-flag-1.1726916094580); [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/636088/SAUDI-ARABIA/Misuse-of-national-flag-for-marketing-purposes-prohibited)
- Industry commentary says National Day campaigns are formulaic ("logo turned green, stock skyline") and should treat the theme as a springboard — [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/fetoun-almuzaini-on-beyond-the-green-logo-why-saudi-national-day-needs-a-creative-rethink/)
- Founding Day symbolism draws on the flag, palm, falcon, Arabian horse and market (souq) — [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/home/article/3489996/saudi-founding-day-logo-symbols-expressing-details-era); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2464026)
- **Privacy and the phone:** the Anti-Cyber Crime Law (Royal Decree M/17, 2007), Art. 3, criminalises invading privacy through camera-equipped mobile phones and related misuse. Penalties are up to 1 year in prison and/or a fine up to SAR 500,000 — [MCIT official text (PDF)](https://www.mcit.gov.sa/sites/default/files/anti_cyber_crime_law_en_0.pdf). The law has been widely reported as making it illegal to snoop on a spouse's phone — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/in-saudi-arabia-its-now-illegal-to-snoop-on-spouse-1.2199597); posting others' images or private information is also a cybercrime risk — [Tamimi & Co.](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/cyber-crimes-committed-by-social-media-users-in-saudi-arabia)
- Influencer landscape: Saudi influencer marketing is platform-specific (Snapchat for daily-life creators, TikTok for entertainment) — [RankRush KSA](https://rankrushksa.com/blog/social-media/influencer-marketing-saudi-arabia/); [Influencer Marketing Hub 2026 guide](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-in-saudi-arabia-guide/). An unverified claim attributed to Kolsquare says the commercial influencer base shrank about 35% after Mawthooq.

### Inferences
- **Lost-phone framing:** make the phone clearly belong to a fictional persona (for example, a chef, a delivery rider or an "AI-powered" food critic). Present it as found and shared with permission, or as a phone the character deliberately planted. Avoid content that normalises going through a real person's phone (spouse or partner snooping is a hot-button topic). Do not show private photos of women or family members, and avoid romantic or relationship storylines. Family, friendship, food and mystery plots are safer.
- Humor: light, self-aware Saudi-dialect comedy and food-related jokes suit the mechanic. Avoid religious references and mockery of national symbols. This is an inference: no source on Saudi humor styles was retrieved.
- Timing: a Ramadan launch maximises order uplift but competes with heavy clutter. Founding Day (Feb 22, just before or overlapping Ramadan in 2027) offers a heritage-themed puzzle hook (souq, Diriyah) that stays within the rules on symbols.

### Gaps
- I found no formal research on Saudi humor preferences, or on attitudes to ARG and fiction-reality blurring formats.
- No GAMR-specific content-standards text was retrieved: depiction of genders, family norms, music and so on.
- Exact 2027 Ramadan and Eid dates were not researched. By the lunar calendar Ramadan 2027 begins around early-to-mid February 2027, which needs checking.

## 5. Regulations: Mawthooq, PDPL, prize competitions, advertising disclosure

### Takeaway
Four regimes apply:
1. Anyone paid to promote aimenu to Saudi audiences needs a GAMR Mawthooq licence.
2. Any personal data collected in the campaign (phone numbers, names, puzzle answers linked to accounts) falls under the PDPL. Marketing use requires documented consent and opt-out, and SDAIA is actively enforcing.
3. A prize competition needs a commercial-competition licence before launch. It cannot require purchase, since that counts as a lottery and gambling is banned. Prizes must be delivered within 7 days.
4. Sponsored content must be disclosed.

### Cited Findings
**Mawthooq (GAMR influencer licence)**
- A licence is required for creators producing paid promotional content for Saudi audiences, including foreign and visiting creators. The fee is about SAR 15,000 for 3 years, and applications go through the GAMR process — [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/what-is-the-mawthooq-license); [Arab News: GCAM lists new Mawthooq rules](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2176661/media); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-tightens-rules-on-influencer-ads-with-mandatory-licensing-1.1733131320843)
- Non-Saudis are barred from publishing social media ads without a licence — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2099346/media)
- Penalties conflict between sources: up to SAR 5M and possible prison (cited by several sources and a 2022 ministry statement) versus a warning, a fine up to SAR 1M and/or an activity ban (Arab News, Mowthooq piece) — [Clyde & Co 2022](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2022/09/new-social-media-requirements-for-saudi-arabia); [Arab News](https://arab.news/g5dhn). Confirm with counsel.
- Disclosure: sponsored content must be clearly disclosed, and brands and influencers may face joint liability for misleading claims — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-in-saudi-arabia-guide/). The exact Arabic label wording ("إعلان") was not confirmed from an official source.

**PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law, SDAIA)**
- Under the current Implementing Regulations, sending promotional material requires consent where there is no prior relationship, plus sender identification and opt-out (Art. 28/29) — [Clyde & Co, May 2025](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/05/saudi-arabia-new-pdp-law-consultation); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=SA)
- An April 2025 draft would widen "direct marketing" to all processing for "marketing" — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/saudiarabia/data-protection/1619092/sdaias-draft-amendments-redefining-the-landscape-of-data-protection-compliance); [Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/saudi-arabia-draft-data-protection-amendments/)
- An October 2026 SDAIA draft would require organisations to revisit marketing consent, recordkeeping and CRM processes, and adds a 20-business-day window to respond to SDAIA requests — [Clyde & Co, Oct 2026](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/10/sdaia-publishes-draft-amendments-to-the-pdpl). Adoption status is unconfirmed.
- Enforcement: SDAIA committees reportedly issued 48 formal decisions in 2025, including for promotional messages sent without documented consent or opt-out (single vendor-blog source; verify) — [Captain Compliance](https://captaincompliance.com/education/saudi-arabias-data-protection-authority-enters-active-enforcement-phase-under-pdpl/); [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/saudi-arabia-s-data-protection-authority-steps-up-enforcement)
- Cookie consent guidance under the PDPL for 2026 — [FlexyConsent](https://flexyconsent.com/blog/saudi-arabia-pdpl-cookie-consent-guide/) (vendor source)

**Commercial competitions / prize draws (Ministry of Commerce)**
- A licence is required before the contest starts. The entry method and prizes must be stated clearly, with fixed start and end dates. The draw is held under Ministry of Commerce supervision, and results are announced on the same channels — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/economy/saudi/2214165); [Amlak infographic](https://amlak.net.sa/en/83508/). Sources conflict on whether the e-licence comes from the Chamber of Commerce or the Ministry, so check current MoC guidance.
- Requiring a purchase or payment to enter a draw counts as lottery activity, which is banned in the Kingdom. The Ministry penalised firms for purchase-conditioned retail draws in 2024 — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/641418/SAUDI-ARABIA/Commerce-ministry-penalizes-firms-organized-lottery-retail-prize-draws-to-promote-sales); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-businesses-that-offered-private-jets-luxury-cars-camels-as-prizes-in-the-dock-1.101886626)
- Prices must not be raised during the contest, and prizes must be delivered within 7 days of the draw — [The Law Reporters](https://thelawreporters.com/saudi-ministry-penalises-firms-organising-lottery-retail-prize-draws-to-promote-sales); [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/saudi-arabia-prohibits-lottery-retail-prize-draws-to-promote-sales-2996153)
- MoC referred 44 businesses for prosecution over unlicensed competitions and discounts, with enforcement covering online stores too. The legal basis is the Anti-Commercial Fraud Law — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2571612); [Saudi Gazette](https://www.saudigazette.com.sa/article/645562/SAUDI-ARABIA/44-firms-face-penalization-for-holding-competitions-and-discount-offers-illegally-nbsp); [MoC news](https://mc.gov.sa/en/mediacenter/News/Pages/21-09-25-01.aspx)

### Inferences
- **Campaign design implications:**
  - Make puzzle-solving free to enter, with no order required.
  - If prizes exist, decide them by skill (first or fastest correct solver) or by a licensed, supervised draw. Do not tie extra entries to purchases.
  - Use non-cash, food-credit prizes where possible.
  - Obtain the MoC competition licence before launch, and publish terms in Arabic.
  - Seed the mystery only with Mawthooq-licensed creators, and label their posts as ads.
  - Collect the minimum data needed. Use a separate, un-pre-ticked marketing consent checkbox, honour opt-outs, and keep records in line with the October 2026 draft.
  - Keep all "phone" content fictional, with no real numbers, people or photos. If the phone shows phone numbers or social handles, use reserved or controlled ones, so no real person receives calls (both PDPL and cybercrime risk).
- Skill-based prizes are likely lower-risk than random draws, but whether a pure skill contest still needs an MoC licence was not confirmed. Treat it as requiring one until counsel says otherwise.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve official GAMR or MoC primary texts (the e-licence portal name, application lead time, fees for competition licences).
- The current Mawthooq maximum penalty is unconfirmed (SAR 1M vs 5M).
- Whether the April 2025 and October 2026 PDPL amendments have been formally adopted is unknown.
- The GCC-secondary regimes (UAE Advertiser Permit, UAE PDPL) were not researched.
